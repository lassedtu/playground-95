# Playground 95

This is a webapp which holds a collection of interactive programs to mess around with. In the style of windows 95, naturally. Made for wasting time quietly, e.g. during a lecture: nothing makes a sound, and the boss key hides everything very quickly.

Programs open in windows on the desktop, and from the Start menu (Programs, Games and Settings).

# Programs

## Polaroid Camera

This program simulates a polaroid camera. You can take pictures of yourself through your webcam, apply filters, write messages on your polaroids and download the ones you like! Photos can also be sent to Paint for doodling.

## Paint

A Paint clone with pencil, brush, eraser, fill, shapes and a colour palette. Open images (up to 2048 px) or photos from the camera, zoom in and out, undo, and save your masterpiece as a PNG.

## Notes

A notepad for actual lecture notes (or pretending to take them). Notes are saved in your browser, and today's lecture note is the one the boss key opens.

## Settings

Display Properties like Windows 95's: pick a wallpaper or import your own, and choose a colour theme for the windows.

# Games

## Memory

Flip the cards and find the pairs of Windows icons, in three difficulties.

## Magic 8-Ball

Ask a yes-or-no question, shake the ball and get your answer.

## Minesweeper

The classic, with Beginner, Intermediate and Expert, flags and question marks, chording and best times. Long-press to flag on touch screens.

## Typing Test

How fast can you type? Pick a text or type random words, and get your words per minute and accuracy.

## Word Guess

Guess the five-letter word of the day in six tries, with statistics, streaks and a shareable result.

## 2048

Slide the tiles and merge them until you reach 2048, with Windows icons on the tiles.

## Reaction Test

Click as soon as the screen turns green, and find out if you're a grandpa or Bill "Microsoft" Gates himself.

## Lecture Bingo

A bingo card of things that always happen in lectures. Mark them as they happen, and share your card's code so friends can play the same one.

## Hangman

Guess the word one letter at a time before the hangman is finished.

## Flag Quiz

Name the country from its flag, either by picking from multiple choice or by typing the name, with a progress bar showing how far you are.

## Traffic Jam

A sliding-block puzzle: slide the cars and trucks out of the way to get the red car out of the parking lot. 200 levels from Beginner to Expert, a daily puzzle you can share, hints and undo.

## 3-4-Go!

97 year old kart racer drawn the old-fashioned way: a flat map tilted into 3D (Mode 7). Four tracks, Hill Climb, Winterland, Beach Day and Westico, each with its own scenery wrapping round the horizon. Pick a car and race up to 7 CPU drivers, each with their own personality, or go for a time trial. Drift for a little extra speed, mind the grass (and the sea), and beat your best lap. Race your friends online in a lobby (share its code, with an optional password), and chase the global top 10 for each track. Works with the keyboard or on-screen buttons on a touch screen.

# Boss Key

Press Esc twice (or pick Boss Key in the Start menu) and the desktop turns into a very serious document of lecture notes. Press Esc twice again to come back.

# Development

Built with Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and React95, using pnpm.

```sh
pnpm install
pnpm dev
```

Traffic Jam's levels and vehicle sprites are generated by the scripts in `scripts/traffic-jam/` (see the comments at the top of each). They only need to be run again to change the levels or vehicles. The same goes for 3-4-Go!'s tracks, skies and car sprites in `scripts/three-four-go/` (the cars are rendered with Blender). 3-4-Go!'s Debug menu (hitboxes, CPU whiskers, the logic layer) only shows up when running locally with `pnpm dev`.

## 3-4-Go!'s race server

Online play needs a small server on Cloudflare (Vercel can't keep WebSockets open). It's in `workers/three-four-go/`: one Worker with Durable Objects for the lobbies, the lobby list and the records. The only things it keeps are lobbies while they're open and the global records (names, cars and times); no accounts or addresses. It runs on Cloudflare's free Workers plan; with no card on the account, going over the daily free limits just makes online play fail until midnight UTC, it never costs anything.

Run it locally next to `pnpm dev`:

```sh
pnpm --dir workers/three-four-go install
pnpm --dir workers/three-four-go dev   # http://localhost:8787
```

and put `NEXT_PUBLIC_THREE_FOUR_GO_SERVER=http://localhost:8787` in `.env.local`. `node --no-warnings scripts/three-four-go/check-lobbies.mjs` checks the lobbies end to end (it starts a local server by itself, or pass a server's address to check that one).

To put it online (free Cloudflare account, once):

1. `pnpm --dir workers/three-four-go exec wrangler login`
2. `https://playground-95.vercel.app` and `http://localhost:3000` are already in `ALLOWED_ORIGINS` (`workers/three-four-go/wrangler.jsonc`). Add any other address the site runs on.
3. `pnpm --dir workers/three-four-go run deploy`. It prints the server's address, `https://three-four-go.<your-subdomain>.workers.dev`.
4. On Vercel, set `NEXT_PUBLIC_THREE_FOUR_GO_SERVER` to that address and redeploy.

Without `NEXT_PUBLIC_THREE_FOUR_GO_SERVER`, 3-4-Go! is single player only.
